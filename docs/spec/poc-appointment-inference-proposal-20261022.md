## Proposed requirements

**APPT-01 — Separate expensive refresh from the appointment path.** POC-4 continues to require real fitting and updated results when observations advance. Run that fitting in the existing background job path. Population/cohort training and patient posterior conditioning/refitting both belong there. Appointment opens, plan selection and approval must not start or await fitting. An explicit “Refresh analysis” queues a frozen-cutoff attempt and returns status; the physician can continue choosing eligible treatments and advancing simulated outcomes. No automatic approval, fabricated posterior or automatic favorable fallback.

For the minimal PoC, manual pre-appointment refresh through the existing dispatcher is sufficient; scheduled production refresh is later work. A research runner may prepare the declared day14/day42/day84 results in advance. A new patient's lack of a conditioned posterior is visible. Population-only/prior-only predictions may be shown only if separately qualified and explicitly labeled; otherwise current model advice is unavailable. True incremental online conditioning and frozen-population modular updates are deferred.

**APPT-02 — Bind information and artifact identity.** Reuse run IDs, owner/patient/cohort IDs, input/H* hashes, model/prior/source/estimand/runtime versions, diagnostic status, posterior/result artifact checksums and existing timestamps. Distinguish (a) data available-at cutoff consumed by the posterior, (b) prediction decision time and horizon, (c) generation/publication time and (d) current patient/state version. Include only information available at the declared cutoff; wall-clock artifact age is not simulated follow-up age. Display both data-through time and result generation time with current/stale/unavailable status and provenance link.

The default current result requires an accepted artifact matching current reduced H*, allowed interventions, model/source versions and support/continuation policy. A changed date alone does not prove identity, and an unchanged date does not protect against corrected observations. Check current projection hash/lineage as well as time. A later report or correction invalidates the claim “conditioned on current history”; retain the old immutable result as explicitly dated historical information while refresh is pending. Do not silently advance its forecast origin or relabel its 42-day endpoint.

**APPT-03 — Bounded appointment computation.** Reuse accepted stored summaries first. Where existing posterior artifacts permit validated counterfactual rollouts, use the same posterior and declared intervention/policy with recorded scenario inputs and numerical precision. Computing a new scenario must not resample model parameters from a newly fitted posterior. Loading, transformation, rollout and summary computation can still cost time and must be independently measured. Prefer precomputing the bounded eligible pair during background refresh. A slow uncached scenario returns explicit pending/unavailable state using existing job/status patterns; never a partial result labeled complete.

Changing a scenario intervention within the artifact's qualified support is distinct from incorporating new outcomes into the posterior. New covariates, new patient groups, unsupported interventions or changed continuation/support policies require explicit compatibility checks. Without a qualified computation, abstain from current model ranking and offer background refresh where meaningful. Eligibility, holds, consent and atomic stale-approval checks use the latest clinical state even when historical analysis remains viewable. Missing/corrupt/wrong-owner artifacts, failed diagnostics and out-of-support requests cannot fall through to another patient's or an older unlabelled result. Broad intervals and inconclusive results stay visible; successful computation does not establish benefit.

**APPT-04 — Measure usefulness and latency separately.** Provisional engineering hypotheses: current summary/status response p95 <=2s; qualified uncached bounded scenario p95 <=15s; explicitly requested background refresh acknowledged p95 <=2s. These are proposed test budgets, not measured performance or clinician-approved SLAs. Record sample counts and cold/warm distributions; small smoke runs cannot establish production p95. Exceeding a foreground budget yields visible pending/unavailable behavior and a measured limitation, not reduced draws/precision. Retain the prior memo's <=8h background completion only as a provisional scheduling hypothesis to revisit with genuine cohort measurements; retire its <=5min fresh appointment fit hypothesis. No real clinical deployment claim follows.

Extend existing POC-VAL timeliness reporting with queue wait, background fit/update, artifact load/validation, scenario rollout, publication, foreground response and UI rendering times; record machine/resources, cold/warm state, failures, peak memory and accepted-result yield. Separately report freshness lag (current available H* minus consumed H*) and effects of using dated results, without treating stale estimates as current-target estimates. Keep accuracy/interval coverage/width and simulated learning time alongside compute cost. No requirement that a component improves accuracy or speed.

**APPT-05 — Preserve the science and app demonstration.** POC-1–6 and the seven V0/V1, N0/N1 and S0/S1/S2 configurations remain required, with genuine default12 panels and existing exploration/confirmation counts/cutoffs. Preserve actual physician choices, causal simulation response, protocol execution, failure handling and separate evaluator truth. Saved artifacts may support appointments but cannot substitute for executing required fits and studies. Retain all enabled-path diagnostics, leakage tests, carryover/rebound distinction, source-once borrowing, support freeze, missingness and independent review. Failed N qualification and unmeasured joint throughput remain open dependencies. StochTree stays deferred; any one-comparator exception needs a separate reviewed scientific contract and policy amendment.
