# Functioning synthetic PoC amendment

Revision: 2026-10-21. User-directed immediate scope; implementation and results remain unaccepted until observed. This amendment supersedes conflicting full-hybrid G0-G7 sequencing, exhaustive scenario/count requirements, and human-review prerequisites for **PoC acceptance** in the older specifications, method plans, UX plans, requirement map and execution guidance. Those requirements remain the later research roadmap, not silently completed or deleted. Unchanged invariants apply to every capability actually enabled. The [bounded validation protocol](07-validation-protocol.md#poc-val--first-ablation-study) is the immediate study contract.

## Keep, simplify, defer

| Decision | Immediate consequence |
|---|---|
| Keep | Working React/FastAPI/SQLite foundation, owned-patient selection, explicit decisions, persistence/history, existing isolated learner/evaluator boundary and selected PyMC/native NUTS stack. Keep seed control, available-at cutoffs, matched estimands, actual computation, diagnostics and explicit failures. |
| Simplify | One generated-panel workflow, one reference longitudinal generator, two eligible visit-plan alternatives with a common continuation policy, fixed support for visit comparisons, minimal working visit, individual-experiment and support components with independent configuration switches. Reuse existing contracts and ordinary-care paths; enable only the bounded protocols specified below; label other protocols deferred. |
| Defer | General-purpose protocol builders, full SMART/escalation, long-horizon/OPE extensions, BCF/library tournaments, broad adversarial catalog, exhaustive SBC/recovery/null cohorts, cross-platform qualification and additional adapters/infrastructure. Do not imply these engines work because schemas or offline prototypes exist. |
| Later human work | Clinician review, human-factors sessions and clinical validation remain outstanding. They do not block autonomous synthetic demo build/load/test; PoC operation conveys no clinical safety or efficacy. |

Do not introduce another governance, lineage or compatibility subsystem. Reuse existing run IDs, versions, seeds and result storage with only fields needed to reproduce the active computation. Preserve failed evidence and prior accepted work; no wholesale rewrite of the requirement ledger is needed. Mark deferred work as deferred, not passed.

## Functional exit criteria

A clean checkout must launch through the existing supported local stack and let a user complete the following in the browser, with persisted server state verified. A static replay or saved screens do not pass.

| Check | Required observable behavior |
|---|---|
| POC-1 Generate and load a panel | A labeled synthetic-panel control accepts seed and bounded patient count (default 12, supported 2-24), generates new fictional patients, persists them for the current account and loads their panel into the app. Different seeds produce different panels; same seed/configuration reproduces generated content. Existing fixed seed fixtures alone fail. CSV/FHIR import is deferred. |
| POC-2 Select and decide | Select any owned panel patient; inspect current observations and eligibility; explicitly choose an eligible plan and confirm. No top-ranked default approval. The decision, effective time and actual simulated delivery/adherence are recorded distinctly. Reject stale or ineligible decisions and cross-account access. |
| POC-3 Advance and observe | Advance one day or to the next 14-day visit, up to 84 simulated days; generate new observable reports and scheduled ISI with declared recall windows from actual action history. Show timeline, gaps, actions and trajectories. A matched-seed branch choosing a different plan must exercise a different causal response path, even when noise masks an individual visible difference. Reload restores time, decisions and outcomes. |
| POC-4 Fit and update | Run real analysis from observed data at the selected cutoff and display estimates, intervals, horizon, cutoff and diagnostic status. Advance and refit consumes newly available observations. Pending/failed/inconclusive results are visible; old results retain their original cutoff. No canned posterior, oracle-derived estimate or future outcome enters analysis. |
| POC-5 Ablate and inspect | Research view exposes independent borrowing, carryover-model and support-policy settings and the selected comparisons in POC-VAL, runs actual fits and opens comparative results/learning curves. It may also open reproducible saved study runs; cached results identify their run/configuration/cutoff and cannot masquerade as new fits. Truth and recovery diagnostics appear only in the separate evaluator research report, never clinician inputs. |
| POC-6 Protocols, reproduction and failures | Exercise the minimal N-of-1 and MRT/adaptive-support loops described below. Replay a seed plus decision sequence, reproduce generator outputs on the pinned runtime, and compare statistical results within recorded numerical uncertainty. Exercise invalid panel input, failed fit, leakage/cutoff guard, stale decision and read-only history after later observations. Verify keyboard operation, readable chart/table alternatives, persistence and browser/console errors for this loop. |

Use an uninterrupted reference setting first; existing interruption/hold guards remain enforced and no outcome is generated after death. Do not invent active interruption modeling to populate screens. A minimal consented reversible N-of-1 schedule must randomize and advance through blocks, record delivered actions and return an actual fitted result; keep support frozen throughout its blocks/washouts. A separate eligible patient must support MRT assignments, logged pre-outcome probabilities, actual WCLS estimates, and bounded adaptive support with missing-reward handling and burden limits. These are required working representatives, not engine_unavailable screens. Durable behavioral care may continue as ordinary care; randomized escalation is explicitly deferred because it adds a separate strategy estimand and protocol beyond these first representatives. Human-entered choices in the interactive demo are for exploration; they are not the randomized recovery dataset or evidence that observational confounding has been solved.

## Analytical slice and implementation queue

Implement a small actual longitudinal Bayesian outcome model and posterior g-computation for the existing [42-day visit-plan contrast](01-estimands-and-contracts.md#4-estimands-before-algorithms), with a declared common continuation policy (fixed support in visit-effect comparisons). Select two already eligible plans with a compatible synthetic external-study contrast; do not borrow behavioral-study effects into medication contrasts. The endpoint remains scheduled ISI, with daily reports as distinct measurements, not interchangeable endpoints. The implementation records its restricted DGP/model parameterization and assumptions before fitting; reduced supported scope is explicit, not a claim to implement the complete reference engine.

1. **Connect generation to the existing app:** implement a seeded panel producer, load endpoint/control and simulation advancement consuming actual decisions; integrate observable export, persisted clock and trajectory UI. Keep evaluator truth inaccessible to the learner. Demonstrate POC-1 to POC-3 before adding more schema-only work.
2. **Connect real methods:** implement the restricted visit model and compatible borrowing switch, actual fitting/rollouts and result statuses; a minimal randomized N-of-1 model with independently enabled carryover term; and MRT/WCLS plus a bounded Bayesian support selector with fixed/MRT/adaptive modes. Validate analytic/simple cases, the borrowing marginal bridge and final-fit diagnostics. Connect POC-4 and the minimal protocol loops in POC-6; freeze support during N-of-1, keep medication actions outside the bandit, and enforce component-specific consent. General protocol configuration and randomized durable escalation remain later work.
3. **Run the bounded study:** add the selected configurations and research results view, execute exploration, freeze a justified confirmation budget and run fresh confirmation. Connect POC-5 and report accuracy, uncertainty and timeliness without requiring an improvement outcome.
4. **Verify and hand off:** independent review and browser exercise of POC-1 through POC-6, clean-checkout launch and a concise reproducible report with commands, seeds, versions, failures and limitations. No enormous archive or new release bureaucracy.
