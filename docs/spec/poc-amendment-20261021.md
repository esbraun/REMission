**APPT-02 — Information and artifact identity.** Reuse existing run, owner/patient/cohort IDs, current reduced H* hash and lineage, input versions, model/prior/source/estimand/runtime versions, support/continuation policy, diagnostic status, artifact checksums and timestamps. Distinguish posterior data available-at cutoff, prediction decision time/horizon, artifact generation/publication time and current patient/state version. A current result must match current H* and its qualified intervention/policy support. Dates alone cannot establish freshness: same-time corrections change identity. Later reports/corrections make an earlier result explicitly dated historical information while refresh is pending. Never silently move its forecast origin or relabel its 42-day endpoint. **APPT-03 — Bounded saved-artifact computation.** Reuse accepted stored summaries first and prefer precomputing the bounded eligible pair during background refresh. New outcomes, covariates, groups, unsupported interventions or changed policies require compatibility checks and background refresh where meaningful. Missing, corrupt, incomplete, wrong-owner or diagnostically failed artifacts and unsupported requests abstain from current model ranking. Slow uncached scenarios expose pending/unavailable status through existing patterns; partial outputs never appear complete. No new serving stack, registry, scheduler, store or framework.

Latest eligibility, holds, consent and atomic stale-approval checks remain authoritative while historical estimates are viewable. Broad intervals and inconclusive results remain visible; computation does not establish benefit. Missing advice must preserve the physician's explicit eligible-choice, confirmation, outcome-advance and immutable-history workflow.

**APPT-04 — Accuracy, freshness and latency.** The authoritative stage measurements and provisional engineering hypotheses live in [POC-VAL](07-validation-protocol.md#appt-04--appointment-timeliness-and-freshness). No appointment-fit budget or clinical SLA is introduced.

**APPT-05 — Preserve science and demonstration.** POC-1–6, genuine default12 panels, all seven V0/V1, N0/N1 and S0/S1/S2 configurations, existing exploration/confirmation counts/cutoffs, physician choices, causal simulation response, enabled protocol execution, diagnostics and independent evaluator truth remain required.
