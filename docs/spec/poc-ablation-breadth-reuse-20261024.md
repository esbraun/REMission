# PoC ablation breadth and reusable harness requirements

Revision 2026-10-24. **User requirements recorded; amendment candidate awaiting different Astra critique and adoption. No implementation, study design, runtime qualification or result is accepted here.** This is the canonical home for POC-BREADTH-01–03 and POC-HARNESS-01–02. It extends [POC-VAL](07-validation-protocol.md#poc-val--first-ablation-study) and the [seven-condition plan](../implementation/poc-truth-ablation-plan-20261022.md), with work tracked in its [existing ledger](../implementation/poc-truth-ablation-task-ledger-20261022.json). The [intake/source map](../implementation/poc-ablation-harness-intake-20261024.md) records the user instructions and bounded prior inventory; it is not technical acceptance.

The user requires robust synthetic device-like sleep, EMR clinical, demographic and treatment data, analytics able to use appropriate covariate breadth, and a modular reusable testing harness for successive analytics iterations. These requirements must shape the DGP and analytics before they are built or frozen for the study. Existing reduced-profile work remains useful within its declared version and scope; it does not meet this broader requirement by itself.

## POC-BREADTH-01 — Four source families and a defensible joint process

The study must generate all four minimum families as actual synthetic source events, with meaningful variation and declared analytic roles:

| Required family | Design obligation and observable acceptance |
|---|---|
| Oura-like device sleep | Generate a compact set of device-estimated sleep observations with event, availability/sync and quality/missingness semantics. Keep device estimates, patient reports, latent sleep and ISI distinct; a renamed daily quality score is insufficient. Test measurement disagreement and unavailable-device periods without silently imputing truth. No real Oura account or hardware is required. |
| EMR clinical features | Select a compact clinically motivated set of baseline and time-varying clinical observations/exposures with units, timing, source lineage and availability. Context strings alone do not satisfy this family. Specify their roles in prognosis, treatment eligibility/assignment, outcome measurement or effect modification, and what is intentionally outside the supported region. |
| Patient demographics | Generate a prespecified small demographic set with population distribution, dependence with other observed features, missingness/availability where applicable and intended role. Do not infer demographics from fictional names or claim coverage from an unused generic covariate field. |
| Treatment features | Preserve assignment versus delivered/taken exposure, adherence, protocol/policy, dose/schedule where selected, and history needed for carryover/rebound/support burden. Trace these through actual treatment-response descendants; merely storing treatment labels is insufficient. |

Before implementation, the detailed Astra design must specify a compact temporal causal graph/factorization, equations, units, initial conditions, distributions, scenario contrasts and provenance under DGP-02/TIME-01. It must address cross-family and within-patient temporal correlations, heterogeneity, confounding and overlap, adherence, carryover versus rebound, measurement error, missingness and reporting/source-availability delays as appropriate to each target. State which mechanisms are modeled, stressed, intentionally excluded or identification-limiting and why. Correlation alone is not causal realism. Do not invent clinical calibration or tune a favorable DGP to the learner.

Use the selected PoC reference/conflict/null and family-specific settings as the starting matrix; do not automatically cross every feature with every stress. The design must justify compact stress contrasts that can expose consequential misspecification, including observation/availability failure and inadequate support. Any added scenario/count change needs an explicit reviewed amendment; existing counts are not silently multiplied. Independent reduced analytic cases and distribution/temporal checks complement real longitudinal studies; they cannot replace them. Numerical parameters and thresholds are to be prespecified by the detailed design and critique, not invented in this requirements amendment.

## POC-BREADTH-02 — End-to-end analytical readiness

For each selected feature and each applicable V, N or support consumer, freeze a coverage matrix tracing **generator mechanism → typed source event → as-of-cutoff temporal export/H* → transformed model input → estimand/measurement/policy role → result/evaluation check**. Identify units, observation/availability clock, lineage/corrections, lag/window, missing-value policy, permitted information set and version at every boundary. A represented column or accepted general record type is not evidence of consumption.

Select adjustment sets and effect modifiers by the estimand, treatment timing and causal structure; do not blindly adjust every model for every covariate. Mark intentional exclusions with causal or measurement justification, distinguish prognostic controls from effect modifiers and policy inputs, and avoid post-treatment/collider adjustment or conditioning on future availability. Breadth acceptance requires demonstrated relevant use across the selected analytics, not a blanket assertion that every field is irrelevant. Any unavailable family/consumer cell remains an explicit implementation gap and prevents claiming the corresponding broader study is complete.

Version changed generator laws/scenarios, source contracts, projections/H*, feature dictionaries, models/priors/configurations, estimands and evaluator bindings where semantics change. Do not widen an old history reader or executable-admission tuple silently. For any new H*, independent truth conditions on the same complete lawful information set; a reduced old reader is not a generic substitute. Future saved/ingested/appointment results must carry and agree on these identities through existing service paths. Genuine fitting, posterior computation, WCLS and actual adaptive updates remain required for their configurations. Fixtures and injected success flags establish neither analytical availability nor admitted saved-result custody.

## POC-BREADTH-03 — Truth and observed qualification

Keep the stochastic scientific law, independent full-H* conditioning, endpoint/intervention definitions and separate numerical error budget required by TRUTH-01/02. Broader shared causes or latent dependence may require new joint conditioning; do not assume the old patient factorization persists. Prespecify this in the detailed design. Generation seeds, realized latent states and future outcomes remain denied to learners and conditional-truth conditioning. Separately labeled privileged-oracle diagnostics cannot substitute for the comparator.

Preserve the unresolved literal-implementation assumption/T2 bridge, finite-grid discrepancy, full-conditioning, endpoint, matched sampling-SE and numerical qualification obligations. A claim-label/admission contract change is not their solution. New laws/features require their own applicable truth checks and qualified runtimes; historical reduced-law evidence retains its original scope. Scientific uncertainty, estimator uncertainty and between-panel uncertainty remain separate. Failed fits, unavailable inputs, undefined truth, diagnostic failure and abstention are recorded, not scored as zero error or successful coverage.

## POC-HARNESS-01 — Reusable offline study seams

Extend existing research producers, versioned contracts, evaluator isolation, run artifacts and CLI tooling into a modular reusable harness for successive analytics versions. Modularity is required, not an optional one-off script; the minimal PoC design is a small explicit set of callable/file boundaries, not a generic plugin engine, new registry, backend, service, distributed scheduler or training UI. Detailed interfaces belong to the queued design, with these required seams:

1. A frozen study/scenario manifest identifies law and source-event versions, feature/H* projection, estimand, component/analytics/model/prior versions, truth method/claim and numerical settings, runtime/source identity, pairing keys, seed namespaces, cutoffs and policy schedules. Human-readable names alone cannot authorize execution or reuse.
2. A preparation seam generates and exports immutable observed inputs once where pairing requires sameness, with external sources frozen once per scenario. Version-specific analytics adapters consume only permitted inputs through existing qualified analysis paths. Replace a component/analytics version by explicit configuration without editing generic run accounting or the DGP to favor it; incompatible inputs/targets refuse or are explicitly noncomparable.
3. The independent evaluator seam consumes the declared lawful H* and distributional law, never learner posterior as truth. Keep evaluator artifacts/latent diagnostics outside normal learner and clinician inputs. Existing evaluate-one/run-row seams are candidates for reuse, not evidence that this isolation or a common harness already works.
4. One manifest-driven offline CLI provides reproducible prepare/run/resume/report stages over existing artifacts. Freeze attempted units before dispatch, append immutable outcomes, and retain not-started, failed, inconclusive, unavailable and abstained units. Resume never overwrites a failed attempt or searches for replacement seeds; any authorized reattempt is separately identified and cannot erase its original denominator. Runtime admission, exact operational authority and bounded custody remain external prerequisites, not self-certified manifest flags.
5. A report/comparison seam validates compatibility and computes version-to-version accuracy, uncertainty, yield and timing/regression comparisons with all denominators and paired-unit identities. Save machine-readable outcomes plus a concise readable report; existing research views may display them. A new UI is not required to prove harness reuse.

## POC-HARNESS-02 — Meaningful comparisons over time

Retain all seven configurations V0/V1, N0/N1, S0/S1/S2 and the existing selected scenarios, 12 patients per panel/cohort,84-day horizon, cutoffs, 3-to-8 exploration and30-to-100 confirmation bounds. V/N comparisons use identical observed inputs/targets/cutoffs and independent recorded fit seeds. Policy comparisons pair exogenous whole-cohort streams while permitting action-dependent histories to differ; they do not force identical post-treatment data. Fit, generation, evaluator and component-extension seed namespaces are explicit and stable under unrelated component changes.

Each analytics iteration has a versioned configuration and result schema with failure/status denominators, independent truth/precision status, per-family recovery and prediction metrics, policy outcomes/burden, stage timing, memory, runtime/cache/load conditions and accepted-result yield. Preserve APPT freshness/foreground latency separately from fitting and evaluator cost. Regression comparison must identify input/estimand/DGP/runtime incompatibility rather than pooling unlike targets or claiming a cross-family winner. Timing under uncontrolled background work is not a qualified performance comparison.

Reuse frozen development cases for regression detection. Record their exposure to iterative tuning. Each confirmatory revision needs its own prespecified protocol and fresh independent confirmation namespace; repeatedly optimizing on an old confirmation set turns it into development evidence. Preserve prior reports and failures with their original versions and statuses. No improvement or superiority is mandatory; faithful computation, honest uncertainty and reproducibility are.

The reuse demonstration belongs to development/pilot work under the existing exploration bounds; it is not an automatic extra study matrix or second pilot. The detailed design must state its attempted-run budget and how it fits those bounds. Any later analytics iteration needing a new confirmation protocol is separately scoped and authorized, never silently added to the first study's counts.
