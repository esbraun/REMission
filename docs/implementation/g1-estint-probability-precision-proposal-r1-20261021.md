## Probability-specific matched pilot: explicit proposed exception

**This subsection is a proposed narrow addition/exception, not a reinterpretation of the accepted per-trial-minimum rule.** That rule remains unchanged for ISI, quality, source-shift calibration and their existing comparisons. Applying a minimum of every individual plug-in Bernoulli SE to probabilities routinely returns zero for finite rare-event arms even when the estimator's actual sampling variance is positive. A positive replacement constant would be unjustified. Instead, estimate the matched sampling uncertainty directly from the required independent trials.

Run1,000 independent randomized comparison trials for each required n/configuration/history, in the existing disjoint pilot namespace, retaining every trial and its complete arm roster, event indicators, counts, arm-mean probabilities and seeds. Use actual catalog comparison sample sizes; unconditional interruption cells use their declared n (no substitution by smoke preset), and the accepted conditional comparison uses total60 independent conditional-environment continuations,30per arm. Required n240 cases remain required wherever the manifest calls for them. No new production study sample size or reduction in pilot repetitions is authorized. Keep source-shift-scale pilots completely separate.

For arm/component c let Y_j be its indicator mean in pilot trial j, J=1000. Define S_c = sample SD(Y_1,...,Y_J), the empirical sampling SD of exactly that matched per-arm estimator. Retain mean, S_c, each trial's plug-in SE (including zeros) and the within-/cross-arm covariance. Estimate uncertainty of S_c by delta method: u_j=(Y_j - mean(Y))^2; MCSE(S_c) = sample SD(u_j)/(2*S_c*sqrt(J)). This is an explicitly labeled large-sample pilot approximation, not an exact confidence guarantee. Require S_c>0 and finite MCSE; define lower binding L_c=S_c-1.645*MCSE(S_c), requiring L_c>0. If all event counts are zero/one, S_c=0 and this pilot is unresolved; do not remove those trials or supply a floor. The minimum over all required matched configurations' positive L_c supplies S_min; any required nonpositive/undefined L_c blocks the shared binding. Later actual smaller matched sampling SE tightens it. A later genuinely zero target SE still requires analytic truth or unresolved status.

For IID Bernoulli arms, including conditional arms generated by the independent-environment construction below, additionally compare S_c with sqrt(p_hat*(1-p_hat)/n_arm) using the pooled independent pilot roster for that arm/configuration/level only. Retain a95%binomial interval [l,u] for p and the conservative analytic SE lower bound sqrt(min(l*(1-l),u*(1-u))/n_arm); include that positive lower bound in the minimum. A zero lower bound is unresolved. Use a two-sided exact Clopper-Pearson interval for this binding; retain Wilson as the reporting diagnostic. This is an uncertainty limit on the finite-level sampling law, not proof of exact posterior integration. Material disagreement between empirical and analytic sampling dispersion requires investigation, not choosing a looser gate. Fixed declared n_arm is mandatory: do not pool denominators across configurations, levels or arms, or substitute total n for n_arm. Randomize treatment labels with exact balanced allocation within each trial; varying arm counts are unsupported by this proposal and require a separately reviewed matched-variance formula.

### R1 conditional pilot independent units and refinement

For each fixed history H and configuration, construct exactly60 independent conditional environments per trial, then assign exactly30 to each arm by an independently keyed balanced random permutation. For every environment i in every trial j at particle level N, regenerate its entire randomized posterior filter from H with its own independent filter key. Draw **one** posterior state/path from that filter using a distinct selection key and **one** continuation under its assigned action with a distinct rollout key. All three probability indicators for this continuation share that patient's path; do not generate separate patients for the three components. No two environment IDs may share a randomized filter, ancestry bank, selection stream or continuation innovations. A common deterministic H, parameters, quadrature calculation and code are permissible. A common randomized posterior bank, including a bank of32 filters sampled repeatedly, is not. Independent filters only between trials do not satisfy this requirement.

Key identity includes protocol/version, pilot phase, configuration/history hash, trial j, environment i, numerical level, process (filter/proposal/resampling, posterior selection, continuation, allocation) and subdraw identity; map these into the existing keyed RNG contract without adding a new learner namespace. Preserve root seed, PRNG/version, actual filter-run identity, ancestry/seed hashes, selection/rollout provenance and the complete allocation roster. Verify independence by producer execution/provenance and key uniqueness, not merely unique row labels. Duplicate numerical outcomes can legitimately occur and are not themselves evidence of dependence. Trial identities and environment identities are independent across the1,000 trials; never retry or drop a failed/zero-event trial. A missing or reused random filter realization makes this pilot unresolved before S_c can bind truth precision.

The randomized pilot uses assigned outcomes only. Optional within-environment counterfactual pairs may be retained as explicitly labeled auxiliary evaluator outputs using declared shared innovations, but cannot be counted as additional independent environments or replace another assigned patient. Preserve component covariance and any auxiliary pairing metadata. Truth batches retain their existing paired-arm design separately. Across numerical levels only, couple the *same* (j,i) using declared common innovations for refinement; this coupling cannot cross different environments or trials. Thus independent paired trials remain the units for refinement uncertainty.

Why this fixes P1: at finite level N let Q_i be the random filter's event probability. Independent Q_i and independently drawn indicators X_i given Q_i imply IID Bernoulli marginal indicators with p_N=E(Q_i). Therefore Var(mean_i X_i)=p_N(1-p_N)/n_arm. Variation of Q_i contributes no extra shared-filter term. In contrast, one Q shared within an arm yields p_N(1-p_N)/n_arm+(1-1/n_arm)Var(Q). This design removes the latter term without subtracting an estimated variance. Approximation bias p_N-p still needs all accepted integration/refinement checks; independence does not prove unbiasedness.

Run the same fixed1,000 pilot trial IDs at N=1,024/4,096/16,384/65,536 as needed for two successive passing refinements, with complete independently regenerated environment filters at each level. These are refinements of one pilot, not extra trials to pool or select favorably. The accepted32-independent-filter-per-level integration diagnostics remain a separate mandatory truth diagnostic; they are not the pilot's environment bank. No increase in pilot J or particle cap, and no averaging additional fixed-N pilots to force passage, is authorized.

The pilot quantities receiving refinement are each arm/component's grand mean m_N=mean_j Y_j,N, empirical SD S_N, and final conservative binding B_N=min(L_N, binomial-SE-lower_N). At each of the last two adjacent level transitions require all of the following against the **final** epsilon=min(.001,.1*S_min), separately for every required arm/component/configuration:

- `abs(m_N-m_prev) + 1.96*SD(Y_j,N-Y_j,prev)/sqrt(J) <= epsilon/4`.
- `0.1*(abs(S_N-S_prev) + 1.96*SD(h_j,N-h_j,prev)/sqrt(J)) <= epsilon/4`, where `h_j,N=((Y_j,N-m_N)^2-S_N^2)/(2*S_N)`. This paired influence-function delta approximation has the same large-sample limitation as the original pilot SD MCSE; retain every trial pair and the uncertainty terms.
- `0.1*abs(B_N-B_prev) <= epsilon/4`, with positive finite L, binomial lower bound and B at all three involved levels. This is an additional stability check on the uncertainty-adjusted binding, not a new confidence statement. S and mean checks above retain their uncertainty allowances.

Use only finest accepted-level B values in the cross-configuration minimum S_min, retaining previous levels as diagnostics. A provisional binding sets a working tolerance but admits nothing. Recompute all inequalities at the final minimum; missing/nonpositive quantities, failure of either successive transition, or failure by65,536 means unresolved. The existing quadrature/particle/within-filter/between-filter truth budgets and sum/total-MCSE requirements also pass at this same epsilon. This explicitly checks dispersion as well as the mean; the provenance requirement independently rejects shared filters even when all refinement differences vanish.
