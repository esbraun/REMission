## 3. Independent conditional truth calculation

Let h be precisely the reduced history consumed by the target estimator at its strict available-at cutoff. For v1 it contains quality values/indicators, ISI totals/recall, assignments, typed deliveries and lineage, **not** function, diary, name, raw envelopes, seed, or other public-but-excluded data. Cohort H* includes ordered members; under this fixed DGP their latent draws are independent, so the target patient's conditional truth factorizes from other members. It does not acquire the learner's inferred hierarchy or external-study posterior. If a future DGP actually shares unknown random parameters, integrate those parameters jointly with all lawful observations under that DGP instead.

Use the conditional law

`p(b,r,e[-13:t],unobserved deliveries | h, theta_DGP) proportional to p(b)p(r) product p(e_s|e_(s-1)) product p(observed measurements_s|states) product p(observed delivery/missingness_s|parents)`.

Here `b~Uniform(16,22)`, `r~Uniform(.8,1.2)`, `e_-14=0`, `e_s=.55 e_(s-1)+.8 innovation_s`; b/r are random patient parameters to integrate, not known realized constants. Lawful evaluator inputs are the frozen **distributional** DGP constants, scenario coefficients, initial-state distribution, observation law, history projection and intervention/policy specification. Knowing the synthetic law is legitimate; supplying the realized b/r, residual path, missing future reward, patient generation seed/index, or exogenous draws to condition h is forbidden. A privileged realized-latent oracle may be a separately typed diagnostic, never the H*-truth comparator. Structural effects 2r/4r or 1.5r/2.5r are not endpoint contrasts.

Reconstruct exposures from typed observed deliveries, preserving all missing-report nights: `C_s=.65 C_(s-1)+.35 I(taken continue)`, `E_s=.8 E_(s-1)+.2 I(taken escalate)`. Reject unresolved delivery status in the currently complete-delivery profiles rather than treating it as not taken. Start at the public origin -14, never restart at a review. Burden is `b+.55 sin(2*pi*s/21)+e_s-2r C_s-4r E_s`. For v2 add `-1.5r M_A-2.5r M_B+R`, where `M_j=.5 M_j(previous)+.5 I(taken j)` and `R=.5 R(previous)+I(previous assigned B and current assigned !=B)`. A missed B dose is not cessation. Support adds `I(prompt_s)*(.6r-.1 previous-six-night prompts)` to quality mean only; it does not change latent burden/ISI in this law.

The likelihood uses independent SciPy Normal CDF/survival interval masses with stable log differences, implemented separately from `methods.measurement`. Quality is Normal(`10-z/3 + support`, .45), clipped0..10, rounded.001: interior bins width.001 and infinite outer tails. Scheduled ISI is Normal(mean of the exact14 burden nights,1), floor(x+.5), clipped0..28; use one total likelihood, not seven pseudo-independent item likelihoods. Missing quality has probability .1 independent of state; observed probability .9 and adherence .85 are fixed known DGP constants. These factors cancel from latent conditioning when independent, but are still checked and future missingness is simulated for policy information. Pending/unavailable records add no fabricated outcome likelihood. Corrections and strict cutoff are resolved before evaluation; no future record is conditioned on.

**Primary numerical method.** Implement a bounded sequential importance/resample-move calculation in NumPy/SciPy. Particles retain b,r and the AR innovation path (at most98 nights for fitted histories), plus last14 burdens/required deterministic state. Initialize from the stated priors, advance in public-night order and weight each available rounded observation; scheduled ISI weights use its whole recall window. Normalize in log space; record ESS, resampling and rejection counts. Resample below half the particle count. Rejuvenation must leave the full prefix target invariant: symmetric bounded/reflected random-walk Metropolis updates for b/r with the full prefix likelihood, and Gaussian-prior-preserving elliptical-slice updates of the innovation vector with that same likelihood. No likelihood-free jitter and no static-parameter collapse disguised by large rollout counts. Fix move counts/proposal adaptation from development, freeze before confirmation; impossible/nonfinite weights or inadequate refinement yield unresolved truth. The implementation reviewer must verify these transition kernels and observation ordering.

At each qualified particle, intervene at t and advance **all descendants** under each arm. V retains its assigned behavioral plan for42 nights; N holds its assigned medication seven nights under frozen continuation/no prompt while retaining initial medication/rebound states. Draw new .85 adherence, .55/.8 AR innovations, and applicable reporting/support processes from independent truth keys. Use paired future exogenous draws between arms, never from the patient's realized private future. Evaluate the endpoint expectation, not one future realization. Integrate final ISI measurement analytically by tail sum `sum_{k=1..28} P(X >= k-.5)` conditional on its recall mean. For quality, the analogous finite sum over 10,000 .001 thresholds gives exact rounded/clipped measurement expectation; evaluate vectorized/chunked or use controlled quadrature with recorded refinement. Missingness does not multiply the expected quality estimand by .9. Predictive-report availability is a separate output.

For S1 excursion truth, condition only on history before each eligible decision, intervene prompt/no-prompt for the next night, then integrate r, next state, adherence and rounded quality. Respect the observed prior-six-prompt history and pre-outcome eligibility. For the WCLS recovery denominator, compute the expected estimating-equation solution under fresh complete S1 trajectories: accumulate expected weighted design cross-products and outcomes, then solve the frozen six-column system. With constant .5 randomization and MCAR this reduces to the eligible-decision average excursion; verify that equality independently. Do not substitute the average over only retained observed rewards as an exact finite-sample causal target. Rank-deficient truth projection is undefined.

For fixed S0/S1 policy value, independently advance the full longitudinal population and executed availability/burden rules over84 days. Pair exogenous streams, allow action-dependent histories and availability to differ, and report mean daily quality, observed reward rate, cumulative reward and burden with explicit denominators. For S2 learning performance, replicate **whole cohorts including actual learner updates** under independently implemented environment transitions; the policy learner may be called as the algorithm being evaluated, never as environment truth. Freeze actual failed-update fallback and missing-reward handling. A frozen final S2 policy evaluated on new cohorts is a different fixed-policy target. No unsupported OPE engine is required.

The numerical target is the independently specified probability law above, evaluated on float64 with declared PRNG. The generator uses finite53-bit keyed uniforms/Box-Muller; do not claim exact mathematical identity to an infinite-precision continuous distribution. Reuse its precision descriptor, verify finite-grid/boundary effects against the existing precision interfaces, and include any non-negligible discrepancy in the numerical budget or mark unresolved. Generation keys are provenance only, never a conditioning input or a shortcut to latent realizations.

## 4. Verification, error budget and output boundary

Reuse constants, typed contracts and keyed seed derivation, but prohibit generator rollout and learner prediction/model imports in evaluator calculation modules. One generator/evaluator output comparison is useful and insufficient. Reuse run artifact conventions, not a new registry. Proposed files are `methods/truth/restricted.py` (likelihood, conditioning, rollout), `methods/truth/checks.py` (independent reduced calculations), and `services/evaluator/restricted.py` (small offline CLI accepting immutable files and writing evaluator artifacts). These are planned files, not existing engines. No network/SQLite/browser or learner-private-input path is needed.

Before publishing computed truth, require:

* Analytic no-treatment/no-observation, deterministic exposure, AR Gaussian and one-state one-observation cases; tails/rounding bins at0/10/28; exactly shared14-night ISI recall; nonzero prehistory; baseline posterior update. Short-horizon adherence trees are enumerated exactly. Constant/zero effect and arm-swap identities exercise sign and units.
* Independent numerical cross-check: for a reduced one/two-night model, tensor Gauss-Legendre integration over bounded b/r and Gauss-Hermite integration over AR innovations, independently coded observation weights and ratio-of-integrals. Double orders until stable. For an unrounded linear-Gaussian overlap, use a separate Kalman conditional mean/covariance and analytic future expectation. This cross-check is not a call to the primary particle filter, generator or learner.
* Faults: realized-seed/state injection, extra excluded public field, late correction/future outcome, swapped patient/source/runtime, omitted descendant/exposure/rebound update, wrong retained policy, quality-vs-ISI substitution, counting seven ISI items independently, reporting fraction scaling, collapse with apparently tiny rollout SE. Each must reject or produce detectable disagreement.
* Refinement on real restricted histories as well as overlap cases. Preserve `ConditionalAccuracy`: levels `[1024,4096,16384,65536]`, 32 independent filters per level, at least three levels/two successive refinements. Use coupled truth randomness across levels where valid, independent across filter replicate IDs. Report both signed estimates and absolute differences; paired SEs must come from the actual replicate differences, not particle rows.

Set epsilon=min(.05 ISI or .01 quality, .1*smallest matched estimator SE). For V/N use the smaller positive final estimator SE across the paired configurations; for S1 use its actual clustered SE. Pilot SE sets a provisional target; tighten after final results. A missing matched SE leaves comparison precision pending; zero SE requires analytic truth or unresolved status. Never count missing/failed fits as an infinite SE that relaxes truth.

Existing `ConditionalAccuracy.require_accuracy` allocates at most epsilon/4 each to quadrature change, maximum of two `abs(refinement difference)+1.96*paired_SE`, within-filter MCSE and between-filter MCSE, with sum <=epsilon. Keep these four quantities separately; rollout SE alone does not control conditioning bias. This is empirical numerical qualification, not a proof of asymptotic unbiasedness. Independent reduced checks and particle/move stability are required to detect jointly wrong or collapsed refinements. Preserve numerical scalar batch accounting (10,000 draws per batch; existing cap1,000) and bind actual draw counts, rather than pretending all particles are independent future trajectories. Freeze a wall-time/RSS cap before the first actual history evaluation; exhaustion retains partial diagnostics with inconclusive status. No compute measurement is claimed here.

`private.TruthRequest` currently rejects restricted profiles; `TruthRequestV2` and probability evidence also bind exact canonical artifacts. Therefore add only an explicitly reviewed restricted-target branch/descriptor to those existing request/response validators, plus a reduced-H* reference/hash; do not forge canonical full-DGP bindings or promote schemas by changing a version string. Retain response truth kind, information_set, request hash, source/runtime, policy/estimand/DGP/configuration versions, truth seeds, refinement/precision references, status and checksums. No interruption probabilities are needed for this uninterrupted PoC. Existing probability/filter/pilot receipts become applicable only if their exact probability/interruption targets are enabled; do not claim them from scalar ISI evidence or fabricate structural proofs. If a future target is undefined after death/out-of-scope, use the separate EST-INT contract; do not subtract nonexistent outcomes.
