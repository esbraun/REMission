## Inputs, clocks and execution

`poc-visit-v1`, `poc-n1-v1`, `poc-support-v1` identify immutable configurations, with hashes recorded through existing VersionRef. Allowed inputs are typed, available observed records and explicitly public protocol/design constants. The law's functional form and fixed decay constants may be declared modeling assumptions; realized baseline/response/error states, generator seed/index, replay actions metadata, private source and evaluator truth may not enter the learner. Derive action/exposure history from typed assignments and deliveries, not simulation metadata. Assignment RNG keys are independent of generator outcome keys. Fit seeds are independent of both.

All patients in a cohort export have the same requested cutoff and must have reached it; otherwise reject the cohort request instead of silently mixing follow-up lengths. Patient-specific analysis may use an explicitly selected compatible owned panel. Research cohorts are segregated by run, not pooled with interactive physician choices. No cross-owner borrowing of local patient rows. Duplicate/corrected observations resolve through existing lineage before likelihood construction; record consumed record IDs/hash and each likelihood block's ownership.

Keep the accepted noon decision /20:00 delivery /overnight response /11:59 report convention. No new TIME-01 clock migration. Strict available-at cutoff applies to nested covariates, rewards and source-study artifacts. Research v2 uses the same clock. A visit result at day84 predicts through day126 privately, while the interactive simulation remains capped84. Retrospective later-outcomes views never become original fit inputs.

Submission saves a frozen request/export, input versions and pending job atomically, then returns immediately. Use one local dispatcher launched by the existing stack tooling: claim a job through a service CLI transaction, stage only the approved export to a separate read-only public input volume, run the learner with network none and a separate output volume, validate/ingest its result through the service CLI. It must not fit inside a SQLite write transaction or approval click. One fit at a time, 2 CPU/2 GiB initially; no distributed scheduler. Restart marks an interrupted attempt failed with its logs; a deliberate retry creates a linked attempt. Repeated HTTP submission with the same key resolves to the same attempt.

Add `analysis_attempts` with job/patient/owner/cohort identity, request/input hash, cutoff, configuration refs, stage/status, result/artifact refs, elapsed times and failure reason. `pending_work` supplies job status; do not overload its reason string with an entire posterior. Implement one explicit migration and update `Store.validate_control`'s exact migration-set check and installation/copy tooling together; normal service startup must not execute migrations. Large draws live in existing artifact storage, not SQLite blobs or Git.

Progress stages are queued, preparing, fitting, diagnostics, rollouts, complete/failed/abstained; do not fabricate a percentage. An accepted result arriving after patient advancement stays at its original cutoff and is visibly stale, never overwrites a newer result. Clinical hold/consent changes prevent assignment regardless of completed fit. Historical analysis is read-only. Submission and result endpoints enforce ownership; context switches cancel display subscriptions and clear drafts, not server jobs.

## Visit model and assignment contrast

Use only the uninterrupted v1 behavioral pair, no medication/support records in this model. EST-VISIT is escalation minus continuation in expected day42 scheduled ISI conditional on current observed history. At time zero assign one of the two plans; the common continuation mapping at days14 and28 is “retain the currently assigned plan.” Thus both branches use the same mapping while retaining their different initial plans. Support is fixed no-prompt. Past delivered exposures are retained, never reset at the new assignment. No superiority badge is enabled.

Fit a small shared-severity state-space model, not independent regressions on ISI items:

- Latent daily burden `z_it = b_i + a*sin(2*pi*t/21) + e_it - beta_Ci*C_it - beta_Ei*E_it` on the complete daily grid, including untreated nights -13..0. Initialize exposures and e at -14 to zero. `e_it = rho*e_i,t-1 + sigma_e*epsilon_it`; use noncentered innovations.
- Reconstruct observed C/E with declared decay .65/.8 and input .35/.2 times recorded taken action; a missing delivery is not zero adherence. The v1 fitted region requires complete typed delivery history. Reject inconsistent histories rather than fill from the private replay key.
- Priors: `mu_b ~ Normal(19,5)`, `sd_b ~ HalfNormal(3)`, `b_i ~ Normal(mu_b,sd_b)`; population beta_C/beta_E independent `Normal(0,4)`; patient deviations multivariate independent normal with each SD `HalfNormal(1)` (no fitted correlation); `a ~ Normal(0,1)`; `rho ~ Uniform(-.95,.95)`; innovation and ISI noise SDs `HalfNormal(2)`. Negative effects are allowed. These are assumptions, not recovery of known private draws.
- ISI total has rounded/clipped normal probability: interior k uses CDF difference over `[k-.5,k+.5)`, 0/28 use the infinite tail bins. Its mean is the average of the 14 latent nights named by that record's recall. Never multiply seven item likelihoods: current items are deterministic partitions of the total.
- Daily quality has mean `10-z/3`, noise SD `HalfNormal(1)`, clipping0..10 and rounding .001. Use the appropriate interval probability, with infinite boundary bins. Function and diary are displayed/predictively checked but excluded from the primary likelihood; do not treat them as duplicate ISI observations. Only available nonmissing quality contributes. Missing nights remain on the latent grid.
- V1 missingness is MCAR by its declared restricted law. Estimate an observation rate from observed indicators for reporting/prediction; do not claim an MAR/MNAR correction. Adherence probability has `Beta(1,1)` prior updated from typed taken/not-taken observations. Future assignments are interventions; future adherence is sampled, not set to one or held at the last observation.

Every update refits cumulative distinct observations with the original prior and the same external-study terms exactly once. No previous posterior reused as independent evidence. At zero local data label prior-only; a numerical posterior is not individual evidence. Limited treatment variation, no local arm support, or insufficient duration is shown explicitly. Physician-selected paths are exploratory conditional predictions under exchangeability/model assumptions, not randomized causal validation.

Posterior g-computation samples patient states/effects, future adherence and AR innovations under both plans, using paired future innovations to reduce contrast MC error. Compute expected rounded/clipped ISI over future nights29..42; integrate measurement noise via its category probabilities. Retain chain/draw axes. Increase inner rollouts until numerical SE of the reported mean contrast is <=.05 ISI points and <=10% of posterior SD (when nonzero); cap at a declared resource budget and abstain if unmet. Never use simulator code or latent draws for these predictions.

## Compatible external borrowing

Choose the already permitted **joint external-study likelihood**, not both a joint likelihood and imported MAP prior. This avoids an additional required R production bridge; RBesT remains later/reference work. V0 has no external terms. V1 uses two independently generated synthetic randomized studies, 120 subjects each, 1:1 plan assignment from untreated baseline, 42 days, same retained-plan continuation, fixed no-prompt, complete scheduled ISI. Generate actual endpoint data and publish each difference of sample means and its sampling SE, study IDs/population/design and availability date. No noiseless oracle effect masquerades as a trial estimate; public study artifacts omit generator replay keys. Use separate study seeds and participants; reject repeated IDs or any IPD/summary/meta-analysis overlap.

For study s, calculate `g_s(theta)`, the **marginal expected ISI contrast**, by integrating the fitted longitudinal model over the declared source baseline/effect distribution, future adherence, process noise and rounded endpoint. It is not beta_E-beta_C: exposure accumulation, recall and clipping matter. Source and target reference population hyperparameters are shared in the reference restriction; no modifiers are invented from aggregate studies. Use fixed Gauss-Hermite quadrature over source baseline/treatment random effects. Conditional on these effects, calculate the recall-average mean/variance from exact linear-filter weights for Bernoulli adherence, AR innovations and ISI noise; approximate the weighted adherence sum as Gaussian and integrate rounded/clipped category probabilities. This differentiable moment approximation is the only proposed bridge approximation, not simulator truth. Compare it with independent forward Monte Carlo from the public model and doubled quadrature resolution, including prior tails; reject borrowing if its tolerance fails. Never threshold fixed uniforms by a sampled adherence probability inside a NUTS likelihood. Require bridge mean error <=.05 ISI points and <=10% of study SE; resolution changes cannot change model comparisons materially.

Use a robust study likelihood `0.5*N(d_s | g_s(theta)+bias_s, SE_s^2+tau^2) + 0.5*N(d_s | 0, SE_s^2+10^2)`, with `bias_s ~ N(0,.5^2)` and `tau ~ HalfNormal(1)` in ISI points. The second component is explicitly noninformative about target treatment parameters. This is a robust joint mixture, not an assertion of an exact RBesT MAP distribution. Preserve normalized mixture constants. A declared sensitivity uses `tau ~ HalfNormal(2)` on pilot fits; freeze the primary before confirmation. Inspect induced marginal contrast distributions and external component responsibilities; do not equate mixture weight with a patient count. At cold start the target cannot diagnose conflict with absent local outcomes.

Reference external studies use the same population/law. External-conflict changes only external escalation burden coefficient from4 to0; sharp-null sets local and compatible external C/E coefficients to0. Freeze these privately before generation. The fitted model and allowed inputs do not receive scenario truth coefficients. No behavioral evidence enters medication or support fits.

## Minimal reversible N-of-1 extension

Add a versioned private v2 generator mode on the existing patient/advance route, explicitly selected when a synthetic protocol is approved. Existing patients remain v1; start new v2 protocol patients through the same panel generator. V2 retains v1 base streams/equations so its no-medication/no-support prefix agrees with v1, with separate added streams. Never reinterpret an existing v1 history as v2. Keep the ordinary behavioral background fixed at continuation and support no-prompt. This is a reversible fictional MED_A/MED_B experiment, never crossover of behavioral skills.

One approved template: eight independently randomized seven-night blocks, each followed by two washout nights (72 days). Each block draw is Bernoulli(.5), persisted at its start before outcomes; not eight independent nights. Review after blocks4 and8 (days36/72); earlier output is unavailable. Require at least two blocks per arm for a fitted comparison, otherwise inconclusive. Record unblinded patient/clinician status. No efficacy early stopping. Hold, death, withdrawal, rescue or background-treatment change pauses/terminates the representative; retain deviations, do not claim the original uninterrupted target remains identified.

Before every block: current `Consent(component=nof1)` with approved_synthetic oversight, clinician approval of both options and template, stable eligibility/fall-risk review, and unchanged background/support snapshot. Existing approval validators remain authoritative. Extend generated-patient eligibility only for the approved v2 MED protocol; do not make MED choices ordinary v1 options. Approval/randomize and advance share one protocol state machine using the existing stage IDs, rather than bypassing their current engine-unavailable checks. Two washout nights are a fictional setting, not assumed complete clearance. During blocks and washout no adaptive support update may change the snapshot. Withdrawal stops future draws without deleting past records.

Private extension to burden: `-1.5*r_i*M_A,t -2.5*r_i*M_B,t + 1.0*R_t`, with `M_j,t=.5*M_j,t-1+.5*I(taken j)`, and `R_t=.5*R_t-1+I(previous assigned MED_B and current assigned option is not MED_B)`. Initial M/R are0. Washout has no new medication input. This withdrawal impulse follows assignment cessation, distinct from a missed dose and from retained exposure. Medication adherence uses a separate keyed .85 stream; existing quality/ISI measurement rules remain. Both actual delivery and assigned schedule are observed; latent r remains private. No real dose or drug claim.

N1 fits the same latent daily grid/quality observation model with inferred medication amplitudes and rebound amplitude (`Normal(0,3)` population priors; patient SD `HalfNormal(1)`), declared decay .5, and AR residuals. N0 replaces M by that night's taken indicator (zero carryover); it retains exactly the same R/rebound model, priors, data and missingness handling. Both retain behavioral background and do not treat washout as clean control. Fixed kinetics make the comparison tractable; kinetics identification is not claimed.

EST-N1 at a review cutoff is the patient's expected mean daily quality over the next hypothetical seven-night block assigned A minus B, conditional on available history, common frozen support/background, with future adherence and previous residual exposure/rebound integrated. It is an assignment contrast, not the fitted medication coefficient. Day36/72 estimates use only data then available. This prospective target avoids using postassignment outcomes as if they had been known at the assignment cutoff. Few blocks may leave wide/prior-driven intervals; that is a valid result.

## MRT and bounded support extension

Use a separate eligible patient/cohort from N-of-1, same v2 clock and continuing behavioral background. Require current `mrt_bandit` consent/oversight and an approved binary prompt/no-prompt envelope. One support decision per night at noon, delivery20:00, next morning quality reward. Burden allows at most three prompts in the preceding rolling seven-night window including the proposed night, and no consecutive-night prompts. A hold/pause denies assignment. If only no-prompt is eligible, record deterministic probability1 and exclude the point from randomized WCLS.

The private quality mean adds `A_t*(.6*r_i-.1*n_t)`, where n_t is delivered prompt count in the previous six nights; prompt delivery is immediate/perfect in this restricted representative. The intervention has no direct ISI effect. This defines a modest habituation setting without adding a new latent support process. Observation missingness remains independent .1; no missing reward becomes zero. All prompt counts and predecision context come from typed records, never future response.

S0 always no prompt. S1 draws .5 when both actions are eligible. S2 uses the bounded Bayesian selector below. Persist full final eligible distribution, availability/reason, policy/fit version, predecision context and outcome-window ID before drawing; existing Assignment stores chosen probability, a small typed support-decision record stores the full distribution/mask. Deterministic/unavailable records are retained. Atomic advance failure rolls back pending assignment/delivery/outcome/clock together; replay uses the same independent assignment key.

WCLS uses S1 only: q=.5, weight q(A)/p(A), regress quality on controls `[1, prior available quality (default7), missing-history indicator, n_t, t/84]` and `(A-q)` with intercept-only effect basis. The target is the average proximal excursion effect among eligible two-action decision points under S1's history distribution. Use statsmodels WLS, participant-clustered sandwich with explicit small-sample/df correction and t critical value with clusters-1 degrees of freedom. Freeze exact options/version. Singular/insufficient-cluster designs abstain; 12 patients do not imply 1008 independent randomized units. Check coefficients against an analytic calculation and offline MRTAnalysis on identical rows; do not demand identical SEs from different corrections.

MCAR is the primary restricted observation assumption: complete observed rewards yield the estimating equation without differential observation weighting. Report this restriction prominently, and a prespecified pattern-mixture sensitivity adding -.5/0/+.5 quality points to the missing-reward conditional mean separately by action. It is sensitivity, not identified imputation or an MNAR correction. For the selector, if these settings reverse its preferred action, fall back to fixed .5 among allowed actions. General learned MAR weights and outcome-dependent reporting DGPs remain later scope.

S2 uses PyMC hierarchical action-centered working regression with the same control basis, patient intercepts and patient treatment effects partially pooled about a population effect. Proper Normal(0,2) effect/control priors, intercept Normal(5,3), group SDs/noise HalfNormal(2); use clipped/rounded quality likelihood. Include a latent daily AR residual across missing nights, with rho Uniform(-.95,.95). This is a working policy model, not WCLS inference. Fit only completed available rewards, refit cumulative unique rows every14 days; retain fit cutoff/age. Initial policy and failed/unready refresh fallback are .5, constrained by the same burden envelope. No claim of adaptive inferential coverage.

Estimate posterior probability prompt has higher expected reward from joint draws, ties split equally; clip to [.1,.9] when both actions eligible. Require probability MCSE <=.02 before using it; otherwise fixed .5. Apply consent/hold/burden masks before probability bounds. One allowed action is deterministic. The service applies these rules and persists final probabilities, not raw Thompson fractions. During any N-of-1 block/washout, the stored no-prompt snapshot overrides global refreshes. Medication actions never enter this selector.

## Diagnostics, evaluator and study budget

Use the qualified pinned PyMC/native NUTS/float64 stack and ArviZ APIs from `tooling/library/probe-python.py`; its toy qualification is not a model pass. Four final chains, initially1000 tune +1000 saved draws each, R-hat<1.01, bulk/tail ESS>=400 for reported effects and probabilities, no unresolved divergences. Record energy/treedepth warnings and MCSE. One documented extension to2000+2000 is allowed; unresolved failure yields abstention, not a changed diagnostic threshold. Pilot may revise and version sampling settings before confirmation. No sampler/library tournament.

Prior/predictive checks and analytic zero-effect/no-noise limits must detect ignored data, reversed coding, duplicated likelihood contributions and incorrect 14-night recall. Fit acceptance is computational; small-sample calibration is empirical and may be poor. No calibrated superiority/meaningful-benefit badges are enabled by this package.

Evaluator-only code may use the private law and parameters, but conditional-history targets must integrate latent uncertainty given the declared observed H. A difference conditional on the simulator's realized hidden r/b/e is a different oracle target and cannot be used as truth for conditional-history interval coverage. Implement a bounded known-law filtering/importance calculation over b/r and latent AR paths conditioned on available quality/ISI and observed deliveries; independent batches assess particle/Monte Carlo error. Research assignments are randomized, so conditioning does not require a hidden clinician assignment model. Pair intervention future streams after filtering. Check the filter/rollout against independent simple analytic cases and increased particle budgets. Stop at a recorded resource cap; unresolved degeneracy or truth precision yields truth-unavailable, not false coverage. Require truth SE <=.05 outcome points and <=10% of reported posterior SD where nonzero. Keep truth error separate from posterior MCSE and across-panel variation. Never call the learner's fitted posterior “truth.”

For S1 excursion truth, average known-law one-step action contrasts over eligible S1 histories, including clipping/measurement expectation. S0/S1/S2 value is expected mean proximal quality over the84-day **complete cohort algorithm**, with reporting rate and burden separate; evaluate via paired independent full-cohort simulations, not an adaptive regression interval. Privileged latent-value diagnostics are explicitly labeled and only in evaluator research reports. No new OPE implementation is required.

Execute the seven selected configurations only: V0/V1 at14,42,84 under reference, external-conflict and sharp-null; N0/N1 at36/72 under the one nonzero-carryover/rebound setting; S0/S1/S2 over84 days under one support setting, with S1 WCLS at14,42,84. Visit study behavior randomizes each initial plan .5 and retains it; no randomized escalation strategy is implied. Each family has12 independently generated patients per panel. Pair V/N observations and cutoffs; pair support exogenous streams while allowing policy-dependent histories. External studies are frozen once per scenario; reported visit operating characteristics are conditional on those external studies, not unconditional repeated-trial uncertainty.

Start with3 independent panels per scenario/family, extend to8 only for fit/runtime/variance uncertainty. This means54 initial visit fits,12 N-of-1 fits and9 support cohort runs (plus their declared refreshes/WCLS), not a combinatorial matrix. Record costs before confirming. Choose30–100 fresh panels per scenario/family from measured paired-error SD and explicit desired MCSE; do not invoke the historical20-fit pilot. At30/100, worst-case coverage MCSE is about.091/.05; high-precision calibration and rare-event safety cannot be certified. If resource cost or precision cannot meet the cap, report incomplete/inconclusive with required cost/count; do not silently omit confirmation. Freeze the seed namespace, law/model/prior/protocols, integration resolution, metrics and attempt policy before fresh confirmation.

Report bias/RMSE/interval width/95% coverage and both successful-fit coverage and success-and-coverage over all attempts, with failed/abstained/truth-unavailable counts. Use panels as replicate units; patient-specific summaries account for clustering. Paired mean-error differences use their SD/sqrt(R); nonlinear summaries bootstrap paired panels. Show observed-data counts, simulated days, cold startup, fit, diagnostics, rollout and end-to-end wall time, accepted-fit yield and peak memory. Research plots preserve each family/outcome/estimand, never rank incompatible effects together. Null rankings are descriptive, not superiority certification.
