## 1. Clinical eligibility and executed policy restriction

Keep `eligibility` as the two-action **clinical/consent/hold/burden** assessment. A fixed S0 or N-of-1 no-prompt snapshot must never fabricate a clinical exclusion. Introduce only `SupportDecision` schema2.1.0, with mandatory `policy_constraint = {allowed_actions, reason, snapshot_ref}`: reason is `unrestricted`, `fixed_no_prompt` or `nof1_snapshot`; the latter two require exactly `[no_prompt]` and an exact immutable policy/snapshot reference. Unrestricted permits both actions and has no snapshot restriction. Existing2.0.0 records/readers retain their original validator and bytes; do not add default/null fields to them.

Let C be clinically eligible actions, P the policy-permitted set, E=C intersect P. The service computes and persists the complete final vector **before** assignment/outcomes. Unavailable means E empty, zero masses, no assignment, explicit reasons. Deterministic means E has one action with mass1; randomized means E has both, normalized masses in[.1,.9]. Excluded actions always have zero mass. Existing chosen-action probability must equal its persisted full-vector entry. A policy cannot revive an action denied by consent, hold or burden; paused/hold denies assignment even for S0. Snapshot identity is frozen through every N-of-1 block/washout despite global refresh. Use the existing approved component/Assignment metadata, including deterministic probability1 and its independent execution key; availability, not the presence of a seed or experimental-component flag, identifies randomized points.

Negative cases: clinically eligible prompt plus S0 is deterministic **by policy**, not by clinical exclusion; burden-only no-prompt determinism remains clinically explained; a held fixed policy produces no assignment; forged/nonowned snapshot, contradictory mask/probabilities, p1 labeled randomized, missing predecision persistence or refresh changing an N-of-1 snapshot are rejected. Only S1/mrt, two-action randomized eligible points enter WCLS; S0/frozen and burden-deterministic points never do. S2 remains policy learning, not WCLS inference.

New patients persist that selection; no existing v1 patient is reinterpreted. Seed/index stay service/producer-private. V2 reuses v1 base exogenous streams; added medication/adherence/support and service assignment streams use separate namespaces. Test exact v1 replay bytes and v2 **base outcome/measurement/missingness/time** prefix equality with medication/support absent; v2 schema/source metadata is expected to differ, so do not claim whole-envelope byte equality. Approval still controls activation; selecting v2 does not approve MED/randomization or enable an unavailable engine.

Reuse existing Consent, Approval, Assignment, Delivery, support outcome/window/closure and saved decision/bundle references. Add one fixed public protocol-snapshot record with normal record/availability/lineage header: family, protocol/template VersionRef, exact consent/approval/background/support references and activation time. Known washout/template timing is public design, while future block choices are not exported. Assignment cessation comes from the typed assigned schedule, never from a missed dose. No generic protocol registry or second store is needed.
