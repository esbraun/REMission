Sol's proposed conservative route is acceptable within the existing plan, with the following concrete scope:

1. For unconditional IID probability truth, independently recompute both outer-batch MCSE and pooled Bernoulli MCSE from the retained complete counts. Require BOTH to satisfy the same final epsilon. Preserve both reported diagnostics and the paired batch covariance; do not alter the matched SE, epsilon, unit count, sample sizes or caps. This strengthens admission conservatively without introducing a discrepancy ratio threshold or a new nominal confidence claim. The independent .0001-epsilon counterexample must reject because pooled MCSE=.0008838834764831844, even though its batch MCSE=.000017960530202675507.

2. Both inequalities alone do not fully resolve the accepted requirement to investigate material disagreement. In the absence of an accepted automatic materiality threshold, requiring a separately trusted investigation/diagnostic artifact for EVERY IID admission is a conservative fail-closed interpretation. This avoids secretly selecting a ratio cutoff. The artifact must bind the exact request/target and truth evidence, raw batch/count hashes, independent unit definition/count and the actual recomputed batch and pooled summaries. It must have an explicit resolved/unresolved disposition and meaningful recorded diagnostic rationale/method identity, not just an uploaded consistent boolean. Verify bytes against an out-of-band trusted expected digest/authority rather than deriving trust from the result's own artifact reference. A missing, mismatched or unresolved artifact must leave computed admission unavailable.

3. Such an artifact cannot override failed numerical inequalities or excuse correlated descendants, reused filters, wrong sampling units, missing outcomes or an unqualified producer. Producer/source/runtime independence qualification remains separate and pending. Contract tests may use explicitly labeled hand-authored fixtures and trusted fixture authority to exercise byte/identity guards; these must not qualify a real investigation or producer. No new human/clinical sign-off is implied: the eventual diagnostic may be produced by an independently qualified evaluator process. At this interface stage, the lack of such actual evidence legitimately leaves production admission unavailable.

4. Meaningful controls should include: the independent failing underdispersion example; the same example at epsilon=.001 with missing/unresolved investigation (still blocked by evidence requirement); an ordinary independently generated or deterministic analytic count fixture with both uncertainties below epsilon plus appropriately labeled fixture investigation (wire admission only); forged digest, wrong target/raw-count hash, changed computed summaries and self-declared consistent failures. Independence is not established by deterministic fixture counts. Conditional/filter-descendant truth must retain its outer-batch rule; never apply pooled descendant N to it.

No new scientific threshold or independent plan proposal is required for this strictly more conservative admission route. If implementation instead wants to automatically classify materiality, waive investigations based on a numeric ratio/test, permit an inequality override, or claim calibrated statistical guarantees for the investigation rule, that is an unresolved statistical rule requiring a separate explicit proposal and independent critique. Root should not choose those thresholds implicitly.
