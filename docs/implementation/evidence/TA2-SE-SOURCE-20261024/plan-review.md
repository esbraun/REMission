## Statistical judgment

**The experiment is now specified rather than selected by artifact availability.** Fixed scenario/behavior/external-source convention, pre-outcome member ordinal, cutoff, complete independent12-member cohorts and paired V0/V1 inputs define the replication unit. `A(H,U)` explicitly denotes the implemented finite algorithm. Sample SD across independently generated cohort replications estimates the variability of this reported scalar under that experiment. It does not become SD of the conditional estimation error, same-history posterior uncertainty, a study-mean SE or an infinite-computation posterior-mean SE. The source `services/learner/cohort_worker.py:141–143` supports the selected scalar: mean of the accepted chain/draw expected-effect array, distinct from its SD, interval and predictive endpoint artifacts.

This choice addresses the original ambiguity without asserting an impossible equivalence theorem. The empirical-SE terminology is consistent with the primary [Morris, White and Crowther tutorial, section5](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10066118/1/2019%20-%20Morris%20-%20simulation%20studies%20tutorial%20-%20stat%20med.pdf): across-repetition estimator SD is distinct from Monte Carlo uncertainty of a reported performance measure. That paper does not validate this project's varying-conditional-target experiment or its 10% threshold. Those remain the explicit project-level decision reviewed here.

**The varying target is handled honestly.** The plan retains member H*-conditional DGP truth while the learner may use cohort sharing. `Var(error)=Var(Y)+Var(theta)-2Cov(Y,theta)` correctly separates estimator spread from error spread. SD(Y) can be large because histories/targets vary even when errors are tiny; therefore it cannot itself establish fine RMSE accuracy, coverage or superiority. The proposal does not make that claim: absolute ceilings, required sensitivity/ambiguity reporting and unresolved conditioning/grid gates remain. Constant estimators produce zero SD even with diffuse posterior, and cannot be rescued by posterior spread. No learner truth, realized latent state, generation key, future outcome or different patient's artifact supplies this calibration.

It is legitimate for the named successful-pair scale because selection is prespecified, does not use truth/effect/error/coverage and all failed attempts remain in denominators. It must never be advertised as unconditional/full-population estimator performance. Configuration-specific success samples require their own declared calibration and cannot silently borrow the paired scale. Implementation must enforce accepted whole CohortResult plus member/chain/estimand gates: current worker lines147–152 withdraw every member output after a joint failure, so an earlier partial member file is not an eligible Y. **Including fit RNG is coherent for the fixed implemented algorithm, but not a precision loophole.** With frozen seeds/distributions, diagnostics, numerical budgets and predeclared extension/failure rules, U is part of that algorithm's sampling distribution. Changing those settings creates a different calibration identity; it cannot increase a previously frozen epsilon. Passing existing diagnostic metadata is necessary and does not magically prove zero numerical bias. Separate per-fit numerical-error reporting, genuine artifact admission and all current gates must remain. No added arbitrary Monte Carlo subtraction or new seed-replication experiment is required by this plan. This acceptance does not approve weakening computational settings or using failed/noisy artifacts to raise the scale.

Three to eight cohorts cannot guarantee a reliable population precision ratio; even final30–100 planned cohorts may yield sparse paired successes. The plan states these limits, reports actual counts and SD variability, treats inadequate cells as pending/inconclusive and retains all attempts. Its delete-one-cohort jackknife is a scalar diagnostic, not a confidence guarantee or a replacement denominator. It should not be used to claim calibrated coverage, a precise population SE, or permission to replace failed cohorts. No new fits solely to estimate this diagnostic are warranted.
