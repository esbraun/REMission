## Delivered calculation

`methods/truth/restricted.py` accepts only revalidated `AnalysisHistory`. Its entry boundary rejects extra fields, late/future records, inconsistent correction heads, unresolved/incomplete typed deliveries, wrong patients and unsupported public origin. It reconstructs C/E from the public origin, including missing-report nights. Baseline ISI uses innovations beginning at -13 after the deterministic zero state at -14. Each ISI is one total likelihood of its exact shared fourteen-night mean; quality uses .001 bins and infinite clipping tails, independently implemented with SciPy log-CDF/survival differences. Fixed known MCAR/adherence factors are checked and recorded separately because they cancel from latent conditioning; pending quality adds no fabricated observation.

The sequential filter integrates independent Uniform(16,22) baseline, Uniform(.8,1.2) response and standard-normal innovation paths. It weights available observations in public-night order, resamples below half N, then applies fixed-count full-prefix moves. A bounded symmetric b/r proposal rejects outside prior support; no boundary truncation normalization or jitter is substituted. Elliptical slice updates rotate the complete standard-normal innovation prefix with an independent Gaussian vector, shrink the bracket around zero, and test the complete likelihood. Bracket/time exhaustion is inconclusive. Resampling copies parameters, full paths and ancestor identities together; every subsequent likelihood is recomputed at the current parameters/path.

The 42-night rollout samples the empirical conditional distribution and new paired process/adherence draws, recomputing every exposure descendant. Both future plans are retained at relative14/28, no prompt. At cutoff14 the endpoint is absolute56 and recall43..56. Final rounded/clipped ISI measurement is integrated by all28 survival thresholds; no realized future report is substituted. Reporting is independently integrated and does not multiply the outcome by .9. Truth PRNG streams use the separate truth namespace/patient/H* hash/replicate/process recipe, never generation/fitting keys.

`methods/truth/checks.py` imports neither the primary filter nor generator/learner code. It supplies separately coded one/two-night Legendre/Hermite ratio-of-integrals, joint-Gaussian covariance conditioning equivalent to a Kalman calculation, and exact short adherence-tree enumeration. `services/evaluator/restricted.py` is an offline file CLI; it requires explicit patient, raw and canonical history hashes. It creates no route, backend, store, registry or admission exception, and returns no admitted `TruthResponse`.
