## Decision and scope

For the genuine V0/V1 PoC study, interpret the relative truth-precision comparator as the **empirical sampling standard deviation of the implemented reported point estimator across independent planned cohort replications**, at a fixed prespecified scenario, pre-outcome member ordinal and review cutoff. Do not use posterior spread, within-history seed variation, the SE of the study mean, or oracle MCSE as this quantity. This chooses one coherent sampling experiment instead of leaving an indefinite missing-SE exception. It reuses the already required POC-VAL exploration/confirmation fits; it requires no bootstrap fits, new backend or extra cohorts solely for terminology.

This is an explicit clarification/change of the former *same-history estimator SE* wording: the truth and comparison result still match the exact individual H*, but the SE calibration is necessarily across a declared population of histories, not conditional on the entirety of that same H*. No theorem equates this scale to a same-history Bayesian uncertainty or to the ideal infinite-computation estimator's sampling SD. Independent critique must accept this interpretation before it resolves the canonical field. If rejected, the field remains pending and the critic must identify a specific alternate experiment; posterior variance cannot silently fill it.

Original TRUTH-02 requires numerical MCSE <=.05 ISI/.01 quality and <=10% of the smallest estimator SE, pilot targets tightened by final fits, zero SE analytic or unresolved. TA-T5 specifies the smaller positive V/N final estimator SE and exact matching; POC-VAL distinguishes fit uncertainty, oracle error and dataset-to-dataset uncertainty. These words do not define a repeated-data experiment conditional on a fully fixed history: an exact fixed-model posterior mean is then deterministic. Repeating only chain seeds measures computational error, not sampling uncertainty. Our proposal makes the random experiment and artifact relation explicit while preserving the absolute ceiling, relative factor, min-pair, missing/zero refusal and final tightening. It does not retroactively qualify the prior posterior-scale proposal.

## Exact experiment, target and estimator

Let s identify the frozen restricted DGP/scenario, randomized behavior/retention policy, cohort size12, enrollment/activation design, law/profile and external-source convention; let t be14/42/84 and j the member ordinal1..12 fixed before outcomes. No pooling across s, t, j, cold-start status or incompatible cohort membership. In replicate r generate the entire cohort H_r under s using independent cohort seeds; preserve dependent observations and hierarchy within a cohort. V0 and V1 receive identical lawful reduced observed H_r and cutoff, but distinct independently keyed fit/integration streams U_0r and U_1r. V1's external study input is fixed across replications for the first experiment; a study that regenerates it is a different explicitly frozen s. No realized latent state, truth keys or future observations enter the fit.

For c in {V0,V1}, define A_c(H_r,U_cr) to be the **implemented fixed algorithm**, including its declared diagnostics, finite sampling/integration budget and failure policy. On an accepted completed result its scalar estimate is

    Y_crj = result.member[j].effect.mean
          = average of accepted chain/draw conditional expected-effect estimates.

The algorithm's randomness is included in this deployed estimator, not averaged away by pretending an unobserved exact posterior mean was computed. Existing chain/estimand integration quality gates remain prerequisites; a high numerical error cannot be used to inflate Y's variation and relax truth accuracy. Settings, seed distribution and any predeclared extension rule are frozen, never tuned to raise the SE. Numerical error of each Y is reported separately. We do not claim this definition estimates the infinite-NUTS posterior-mean sampling SD.

The comparator truth is

    theta_rj = E_DGP[ISI_escalate(t+42) - ISI_continue(t+42) | H*_rj],
    e_crj = Y_crj - theta_rj.

This uses exactly the registered reduced member H* information set, retained visits and no prompt. Learner cohort sharing does not enlarge evaluator conditioning; any alternative cohort-conditioned truth would require a separate estimand amendment. Absolute endpoints are56/84/126; no change to EST-VISIT:poc-hstar-v1 or its information set. theta_rj varies across r. Sampling variability Var(Y), error variability Var(e), posterior Var(effect|H), MCMC/integration variance, and oracle numerical variance are distinct: Var(e)=Var(Y)+Var(theta)-2Cov(Y,theta). Neither a posterior SD nor SD(e) can be substituted for SD(Y); RMSE includes squared bias as well. Dividing SD(Y) by sqrt(R) would instead estimate uncertainty of a replicate mean and is forbidden as the per-fit precision denominator.

### Failures and conditional scope

The implemented estimator is partial: a failed/abstained fit has no Y. Prespecify S_rjt=1 only when **both** V0/V1 target-member outputs at that stratum complete with all existing diagnostics and exact matching. Define the successful-pair calibration experiment as the distribution of (H,U) conditional on this declared event, not on effect magnitude, truth, favorable error or interval coverage. The selection uses observed H and independent algorithm streams only; it does not reveal latent truth or change the registered theta conditional on H*. For that conditional experiment,

    sigma_csj t = SD(Y_crj | s,j,t,S=1),
    s_csj t = sqrt(sum_{r:S=1}(Y_crj - mean(Y_c))^2 / (R_pair-1)).

This is a standard empirical sampling-SD estimate for the declared successful-pair algorithm, not an unconditional SE of an estimator undefined on failures. All attempted cohorts remain in the immutable ledger and failure/abstention/undefined and success-and-coverage denominators. A missing member result never receives a scale or truth comparison via a successful peer. A configuration-specific success metric may still be reported separately, but its estimator-SE calibration needs its own declared experiment; it cannot borrow this paired scale. No survivor subset may be described as full-population performance.

At least3 independent successful paired cohort replications are needed for a provisional s for each j/t/s. This minimum uses the existing3-panel exploration, not36 independent patient rows. If too few succeed, the stratum is pending; the already permitted extension to at most8 exploration panels may address documented fit/variance uncertainty, never replace failures or seed-search. Confirmation retains30-100 planned independent cohorts and reports actual paired-success counts. A cell with inadequate successful evidence remains inconclusive, not omitted from the seven-condition study. Existing accepted fits may be reused only if they belong to this exact predeclared experiment; the different served singleton cannot qualify it.

## Operational precision rule and limitations

Compute the provisional empirical pair scale s_pilot=min(s_V0,s_V1), separately for every admitted s/j/t. It is an estimate, not a lower confidence bound on unknown sigma; no normal/chi-square assumption or posterior bootstrap coverage is asserted. Report R_pair, all-attempt counts, the two SDs and uncertainty of the simulation performance estimates. For the SD estimate itself, predeclare a scalar delete-one-cohort jackknife diagnostic: compute s_(-r) on the paired-success cohort sample with r omitted, and report sqrt((R_pair-1)/R_pair * sum((s_(-r)-mean(s_(-r)))^2)). This estimates variability of the reported SD, not an additional estimator SE or a lower confidence bound. It is only an asymptotic diagnostic, potentially unreliable with 3-8 cohorts or nonsmooth/degenerate samples; flag those limitations and retain the raw scalar count/status evidence. It changes no precision denominator, creates no resampled fits and warrants no normality or coverage claim. The3-8 pilot cannot support a reliable population-SE theorem; use it only as the contract's initial engineering precision target. The finite confirmation estimate tightens by the frozen rule

    s_used = min(s_pilot, s_confirmation, any earlier smaller accepted matched scale),
    epsilon_rj = min(0.05 ISI, 0.1*s_used).

Before confirmation is available the provisional target may guide numerical work, but final study truth-precision acceptance waits for the final binding; if it tightens, reassess every associated truth and refine only under a separately reviewed exact operation. Do not increase a tolerance after seeing confirmation, change model/settings, or discard a small positive scale. This deterministic precision tightening is predeclared numerical quality control, not confirmation tuning. Zero SD, missing calibration, nonfinite data, missing/failed matched fits or an unsupported stratum cannot resolve precision; zero requires analytic truth or unresolved. No floors or infinite-SE substitutions. S1's actual clustered sandwich SE remains unchanged for its different target; N needs its separately reviewed analogous experiment/artifacts and is not activated by this V decision.

An optional posterior sqrt(L) remains an independently labeled diagnostic. If a separately frozen extra rule uses it, it may only reduce epsilon after this matched empirical-SE binding exists. It never supplies the binding itself. Truth within/between-filter MCSE and particle/move refinement errors remain separate and obey the existing four-allowance/move constraints, full-conditioning qualification and finite-grid budget. The canonical absolute quality ceiling remains.01 for separately qualified N/support targets.

**What is and is not non-weakening:** operationally we preserve the original absolute and10%-of-smallest-*estimated* sampling-SE rule with explicit experiment, final tightening and more exact pairing. We do not assert s<=sigma, equivalence to any unspecified same-history SE, or a universal guarantee from a small pilot. Such a guarantee was not supplied by the original words. The ambiguity is resolved openly as an empirical simulation-calibrated criterion. This is a scientific decision for critique, not a proof that all possible original interpretations are equivalent.

For the study's accuracy purpose, require a numerical-sensitivity report as well as the ratio. If an independently justified truth error envelope is |theta_tilde-theta|<=epsilon_rj, then for any fixed collection of scored pairs: absolute change in empirical bias <=mean(epsilon); absolute change in RMSE <=sqrt(mean(epsilon^2)); absolute change in mean paired error difference is zero when both methods use the identical theta_tilde (truth cancels). Each separate RMSE still needs its error bound. These follow respectively from the triangle inequality and reverse triangle inequality for the Euclidean norm; they are not empirical claims about the current evaluator. Paired squared-error/RMSE differences do not cancel and must propagate truth uncertainty.

Coverage/sign/ranking are discontinuous: if the truth-error envelope overlaps an interval endpoint or zero/sign boundary, mark that classification numerically ambiguous and report lower/upper counts, not a confident success/failure or superiority badge. Interval width needs no oracle but fit acceptance still applies. The existing four TA2 allowances are empirical numerical evidence, not automatically a simultaneous deterministic envelope; label resulting sensitivity ranges as engineering uncertainty unless stronger bounds are proved. A claim smaller than unresolved oracle error is inconclusive. This prevents large between-history target variation or positive sampling SD from being used as proof of precise tiny effects. Full conditioning and finite-grid errors are not waived by these algebraic sensitivities.

The general distinction between empirical SE of an estimator across simulation repetitions and Monte Carlo SE of a reported simulation performance measure follows Morris, White and Crowther's primary ADEMP tutorial ([Statistics in Medicine, 2019, section5](https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.8086)). The conditional-target experiment and proposed gates above are this project's proposal, not a claim that that paper validates them.

## Required counterexamples and acceptance

1. A data-insensitive constant reported estimator with a diffuse posterior gives s=0: unresolved/analytic truth, regardless of effect.sd or sqrt(L). A single fixed H* with many seed reruns does not meet independent-cohort calibration.
2. Y follows varying theta nearly exactly: sampling SD can be large while RMSE is tiny. Preserve the.05 ceiling and numerical sensitivity/ambiguous-coverage reporting; never call sampling SD an error SD or claim tiny RMSE superiority without adequate oracle precision.
3. Two different configurations/history/cutoff/ordinal or duplicated cohorts cannot be pooled; shared twelve members are not twelve independent replications. Pair before reducing. Constant V0 with variable V1 still yields min-pair zero, not V1-only tolerance.
4. Failed/abstained members, insufficient paired successes, NaN, wrong units, missing expected-effect diagnostics and uncontrolled integration/MCMC error stay unavailable. All failed attempts remain in denominators. Numerically noisy accepted-estimate metadata must pass the unchanged applicable diagnostics, not an SE-driven exemption.
5. A smaller final SD triggers stricter assessment without rewriting old truth records or increasing budgets automatically. An unchanged or larger final SD cannot relax the pilot target. An unmatched historical V1 patient cannot fill this binding.
6. Algebraic bias/RMSE perturbation and coverage endpoint-overlap cases must pass hand-calculable scalar controls. No empirical TA2 allowance is silently relabeled a rigorous bound.
