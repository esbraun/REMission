## Precise scientific decision needed before model/config work

The [analytics contract](poc-analytics-contract-20261021.md), “Visit model and assignment contrast,” specifies a patient hierarchy and an adherence `Beta(1,1)` prior updated from typed taken/not-taken observations. Its N extension says N1 retains the same latent daily grid, observation model, background and AR residuals, adds medication/rebound population `Normal(0,3)` and patient SD `HalfNormal(1)`, and requires N0 to share priors/data/rebound with N1. The “Inputs, clocks and execution” section allows a declared compatible cohort. The [individual experiment specification](../spec/04-individual-experiments.md), “N-of-1 branch,” describes hierarchical pooling across individual trials. These establish hierarchy support but do not explicitly freeze the executable N pooling unit or distinguish adherence probabilities by component.

Resolve both questions with an explicit accepted equation/parameter-axis table:

1. **N fit unit/pooling:** Is each fit a single patient's full hierarchical prior, or an explicitly selected common-cutoff compatible cohort with shared baseline/background and medication/rebound population means/SDs and independent patient deviations? If cohort, identify every shared versus patient-specific parameter (`a`, `rho`, innovation/measurement SDs, adherence, observation rate included). The target remains one identified patient's expected assignment contrast conditional on the declared common H*. Changing the pool changes that posterior conditional distribution even when the target wording stays fixed. The current 12-patient visit cohort worker owns shared source; its implementation is not evidence that N has inherited the same axes.

2. **Future adherence:** Should the continuation background and medication use one common patient `p ~ Beta(1,1)` updated by both typed streams, or independent `p_background,p_medication ~ Beta(1,1)` updated by their respective streams? The private v2 extension declares a separate .85 medication stream, while the visit model estimates adherence and the N extension retains its priors/background. Independent generator streams do not by themselves prescribe the learner's pooling assumption. The common-p option shares behavioral evidence with medication adherence; the separate-p option prevents that sharing. Both change future medication exposure, future behavioral burden and clipping of reported quality, hence EST-N1. There are also unresolved patient-versus-cohort adherence pooling axes. Do not fix future adherence to the private .85 constant or assume perfect background adherence.

Typed delivery ownership must stay component-specific regardless of the accepted prior: behavioral continuation deliveries update the background evidence ledger, MED_A/MED_B deliveries update the medication ledger, and no-prompt support deliveries are not medication adherence evidence. Missing/unknown delivery is not not-taken. Both N0 and N1 must consume the identical reduced H*, likelihood ownership and accepted adherence law; only medication carryover changes.

After acceptance, freeze a complete configuration/model/prior/estimand/diagnostic/artifact identity proposal before affected execution.
