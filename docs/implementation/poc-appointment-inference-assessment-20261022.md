## Correcting the earlier practicality framing

The prior memo at commit `2f15626ec89487eb3670dd30aa44579f250bdef5`, path `docs/implementation/poc-cloud-fit-practicality-20261022.md`, usefully separated queueing from fitting but proposed a fresh-result-within-five-minutes appointment hypothesis. **Retire that fresh-fit hypothesis:** the user intends appointment-time prediction, counterfactual calculation and posterior summaries from validated artifacts, with expensive fitting outside appointments. An explicit refresh may enqueue work while the appointment continues; its completion is not the appointment request latency.

Bayesian patient conditioning when new outcomes arrive is itself inference and can require a refit. Calling it “inference” does not make it a cheap prediction call. Keep four operations distinct: population/cohort fitting; patient posterior refresh using newly available observations; prediction/rollout from an already conditioned posterior; and a genuinely incremental online update, which is deferred until justified and measured. Freezing shared parameters and updating patient effects is not automatically equivalent to the full joint posterior. Propagating shared draws, feedback from patient data, posterior dependence and counting each observation/source once must be specified before such an approximation is proposed.

The accepted measurement is **one single-patient day14 V1 borrowing fit**, not V0 and not a 12-patient fit. ## Compatibility and identification assessment

Our active target is `EST-VISIT:poc-hstar-v1`: the 42-day ISI expected contrast conditional on reduced available history H*, with common continuation/support policy. It is not generic held-out outcome prediction. N-of-1 and MRT have separate targets and cannot be replaced by a BCF CATE column.

StochTree could replace a restricted **regression component or whole single-decision comparator model** after explicit specification. It cannot directly sample the current PyMC likelihood/prior, robust external marginal bridge, or existing latent-state posterior. A custom Gibbs sampler could theoretically couple forest primitives to additional latent dynamics, but that is a new model/inference project, not a minimal substitution or justified current PoC task.

History/carryover/rebound can be encoded as pre-decision covariates or basis terms, but this does not automatically reproduce exposure dynamics, latent-state uncertainty, informative adherence, overlapping recall, or counterfactual evolution under future decisions. Patient IDs alone do not solve serial dependence. Features available only after assignment would leak mediators/outcomes; histories must use the same cutoff/lineage rules. Missing-covariate processing is not a missing-outcome or MNAR model. Replacing the observation law with Gaussian regression or cloglog changes scientific assumptions and needs a new model version and recovery evidence.

External-study borrowing is especially incompatible with a silent swap. BCF propensity adjustment and treatment shrinkage do not implement the V1 compatible marginal contrast likelihood, source bias/heterogeneity, conflict robustness or source-once bookkeeping. Adding trial summaries as patient rows or setting a forest coefficient prior from a marginal trial average is invalid without a derived bridge. A no-borrowing comparator cannot isolate the effect of borrowing against V1; use V0 on the same target for that comparison, and keep mandatory V0/V1 ablation intact.

Twelve patients do not become hundreds of independent patients because each supplies many nights. Flexible treatment heterogeneity may be weakly identified, especially at day14 with few distal outcomes. Repeated randomized N-of-1 assignments can identify a within-patient target under their design assumptions; ordinary physician choices may lack overlap and exchangeability. Random effects/propensity adjustment do not cure hidden confounding or unsupported intervention sequences. Current interactive choices remain exploration; confirmation uses the declared randomized behavior policy. Any comparator must split by patient/panel and time, never random nights, and preserve uncertainty over independent panels.

## Bounded comparator decision

**Defer enabling a comparator.** The older library plan already identifies single-decision StochTree BCF, while the active PoC amendment explicitly defers BCF/library tournaments. This assessment supplies a conditional route to one comparator, not an eighth mandatory configuration or a replacement for the seven existing configurations.

Proceed only if an independently reviewed contract can define one decision per independent patient, pre-decision H* features, the same eligible contrast, 42-day scheduled ISI and fixed continuation policy, with observed follow-up and no oracle covariates. If that restriction fails to match the active personalized history-conditioned target, label a distinct estimand and do not claim an ablation of the existing longitudinal engine. Do not train an early-cutoff model on its own future outcomes. With inadequate external historical training data, a day14 comparison may be unavailable; that is a legitimate result, not permission to pool future visits.

If justified later, use one explicit MCMC BCF specification against the matched no-borrowing reference, on the existing selected reference/sharp-null panels; external-conflict comparison applies only if a valid borrowing model is actually specified. Reuse POC-VAL's exploration/confirmation budget rather than adding a tournament. Freeze feature schema, treatment coding/propensities, priors, release, seeds, draw diagnostics and allowed information before confirmation. Report effect bias/RMSE/coverage/width, outcome prediction separately, all-attempt success/abstention, support failures, fit cost, artifact load and scenario latency. Reference posterior MCSE and evaluator truth error remain separate. Freeze a justified accuracy-loss criterion and latency budget before confirmation; absent precise evidence, conclude inconclusive. Adoption additionally requires exact artifact round-trip tests, new-group behavior, independent methods review and an explicit revision of the single-backend policy. None is completed here.
