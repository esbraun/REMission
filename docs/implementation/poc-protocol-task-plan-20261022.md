## Canonical scientific and operational invariants

P3 additionally consumes the accepted [N cohort/parameter/adherence restriction](poc-analytics-contract-20261021.md#minimal-reversible-n-of-1-extension): joint explicitly declared compatible cohort, one bound target, shared outcome hierarchy/phase/AR/noise, individual independent p_C/p_M/q Beta factors, protocol-relative review with public-origin latent clock, and preserved strict serialized reduced H*. Implement and independently qualify the distinct v2 N model/prior/config identities before executable admission; old boundary fixtures stay non-executable. Hierarchical borrowing cannot bypass the target's two-blocks-per-arm gate.

Implement the analytics contract's exact eight independent seven-night blocks plus two washout nights each (72 days), Bernoulli(.5) block draws, reviews36/72, at least two blocks per arm, unblinded status and no efficacy stopping. Behavioral continuation and no-prompt support are frozen. Its v2 MED law fixes decay/rebound/adherence and distinguishes assignment cessation from missed doses; independently check those equations, including residual exposure in washout. N0 retains the same rebound, latent quality grid, AR residuals, priors and missingness as N1. The target is next hypothetical seven-night **expected quality A minus B given H***, integrating future adherence/residual exposure/rebound/measurement; no latent oracle or future outcomes enter fits.

For support preserve noon decision/20:00 delivery/next-morning reward, maximum three prompts in rolling seven nights and no consecutive prompts; hold/pause denies assignment. Keep the exact private quality/habituation law, perfect restricted prompt delivery, independent .1 missingness and no direct ISI effect. Persist all deterministic/unavailable cases; WCLS uses only eligible randomized S1 points, canonical q=.5 weights/control basis, clustered correction and clusters-minus-one t degrees of freedom. Missing is never zero. S2 is policy learning, not WCLS inference: retain the canonical clipped/rounded likelihood, AR path across missing nights, priors,14-day refresh, joint-draw/tie probability, [.1,.9] bounds and MCSE<=.02. Apply consent/hold/burden masks first; missing-reward action-specific -.5/0/+.5 sensitivity that reverses preference triggers fixed .5. Medication never enters the selector, and N-of-1 snapshots override all refreshes.
