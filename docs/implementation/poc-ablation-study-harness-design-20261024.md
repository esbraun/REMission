## 2. Compact synthetic law: deliberately fictional, jointly longitudinal

All coefficients below are **provisional synthetic design constants**, selected to expose mechanisms, not estimated clinical facts or claims about Oura accuracy, sex differences, treatment efficacy or steroid prescribing. The design's external references support measurement semantics and study organization, not these coefficients. Independent critique may change them before freeze; subsequent changes create a new law/scenario version and invalidate affected qualification.

There are 12 independently generated patients per cohort, 84 study days and evaluator/predictor continuation through day 126. Prehistory covers nights ending on days -13 through 0. For this breadth-law profile, fix T0=2026-01-01T00:00:00Z. Day d starts at T0+d*24 hours for every integer d, including negative prehistory days; named clock times are UTC offsets from that midnight. A device episode indexed d starts at 23:00 on day d-1 and ends at07:00 on day d. Thus the fourteen prehistory episodes end on days-13,...,0; the latent initialization at-14 does not add another retained device episode. This profile does not inherit the historical restricted-law noon origin. Display-zone conversion does not change stored UTC instants or day indices. Demographics are recorded before prehistory. The selected TIME-01 chronology is morning outcome at 07:00, clinical event at 08:00, report recorded at 08:01, decision at 12:00, delivery at 20:00 and next outcome the following morning. A cutoff admits only `available_at < cutoff`; equality is excluded.

### Baseline, state equations and treatment descendants

For patient i, draw independently age U(30,75), a=(age-52.5)/22.5, synthetic recorded sex code B~Bernoulli(.5), s=2B-1, b=19+1.5a+.5s+U(-3,3), response r=exp(-.15a)U(.8,1.2), and device bias k~Normal(15,10²) minutes. The two-code synthetic field is a limited scenario variable, not a general sex/gender ontology or inference from a name. Add its explicit typed source concept; do not overload a numeric clinical measure.

The fictional scheduled steroid indicator S_d is one on cycle residues d mod 21 in {0,1,2}, zero otherwise; its complete planned schedule is known at baseline. It is a binary contextual exposure, not a modeled drug dose. Latent clinical symptom state X and latent sleep/ISI burden Z evolve as follows. Initialize X_-15=0, e_-14=0, all treatment states at -14 to zero and Z_-14=b+.55 sin(2π(-14)/21). For d=-14,...,125:

```
X_d = .60 X_(d-1) + .12 (Z_d-19) + .50 S_d + .20 a + .10 s + .50 eta_d
e_(d+1) = .55 e_d + .80 xi_(d+1)
C_(d+1) = .65 C_d + .35 taken_C,d
E_(d+1) = .80 E_d + .20 taken_E,d
MA_(d+1) = .50 MA_d + .50 taken_A,d
MB_(d+1) = .50 MB_d + .50 taken_B,d
R_(d+1) = .50 R_d + I(previous assigned drug B, current assigned drug not B)
Z_(d+1) = b + .55 sin(2π(d+1)/21) + .60 X_d + .80 S_d + e_(d+1)
          - 2 r C_(d+1) - 4 r E_(d+1) - 1.5 r MA_(d+1)
          - 2.5 r MB_(d+1) + R_(d+1)
```

The transition loop d=-14,...,125 produces Z126 and its treatment/error descendants. For the terminal clinical observation P126, additionally evaluate the existing X equation at d=126 using X125, Z126, S126, baseline and the independent eta126 draw, then the existing P126 measurement/missingness/availability mechanism. This is an observation-state closure only: do not advance treatment/error states to 127 or generate Z127. Device D126, report Q126 and the scheduled ISI126 use their existing equations. Continuation observations retain their actual availability times, including P126 delayed to day127; no delayed observation is backdated into an earlier export.

All eta, xi and measurement innovations are independent standard normals given baseline; all Bernoulli/uniform streams are independent unless an explicit mechanism below links them. Prehistory has no treatment. For each assigned active component, actual taking is Bernoulli(.90-.15 S_d), independently conditional on the known schedule. Assignment, delivery, taking and missed delivery are distinct events. Unassigned components are zero. Rebound follows **assigned B cessation**, not a missed dose. Support action does not enter Z; it affects next-day reported quality directly. N keeps background continue treatment fixed and support off, as in the existing estimand. The graph therefore includes treatment → burden → later clinical symptoms → later burden, baseline effect modification, serial dependence and policy-dependent feedback; a regression that adjusts for future X would change the treatment target.

In the main reference law, treatment assignment follows the existing V randomized retained decisions, N block schedule and S randomized/adaptive rules. There is no hidden treatment-confounding mechanism in the randomized primary contrasts. S2 action probabilities depend on lawful history and constraints; analysis must use actual logged probabilities/availability, not treat adaptive action as unconfounded marginal assignment. Clinical symptoms and adherence are related through S and prior burden; this is not permission to condition a total-effect estimand on post-treatment clinical mediators.

### Observation, availability and lineage

Let `clip` precede rounding; the likelihood includes the resulting endpoint masses. n_d counts prompts in the previous six days.

For the primary stochastic scientific law, round_h means nearest grid point of spacing h after clipping, with an exact tie selecting the grid point whose integer grid index is even: q_h(x)=h round_even(x/h). Use h=.1 for P and device duration and h=.001 for Q. The literal binary64 implementation uses the documented Python round(x,1) or round(x,3) convention on the clipped binary64 value, without a preliminary decimal truncation or alternative half-away rule. These statements distinguish the ideal-real quantizer from its finite implementation; they do not prove equality near decimal half-grid boundaries. ISI is unchanged: floor(mean+noise+.5), then clipping to 0..28, not ties-to-even.
